The person behind the pages

A librarian’s curiosity.
A life of service.

I’m Paul Clark. After three decades in library technology, I’m still drawn to the same question: how can we make difficult ideas useful and accessible to the people who need them?

My books, Cipher Museum, and Crypto Lab bring that question to cryptography—with attention to the sources, space to experiment, and care for the people behind the systems.

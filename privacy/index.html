What this site does

The site does not include an email sign-up form, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics scripts. Fonts and images are served with the site. Site hosting may process standard request information, such as an IP address, for delivery and security.

Contacting Paul

Email links open your email application. Information you choose to send is handled through email. Please do not include confidential information in an initial inquiry.

Other websites

Links to Cipher Museum, Crypto Lab, and other resources take you to separate websites, which may have their own privacy practices.
